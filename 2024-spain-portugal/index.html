Spain to Portugal: Madrid to Lisbon

A BMW R 1300 GS from Hertz Ride in Madrid, south across La Mancha to Valdepeñas, down to the cliff-top town of Ronda in Andalusia, west into Portugal to the hilltop fortress of Marvão, and on through Évora to Lisbon.
